Avatar Creator

An animated 2D character you can rebuild piece by piece: swap the body parts, mix costume pieces slot by slot, and watch every combination move.

The character lives in avatar.js: a small bone hierarchy (pelvis → torso → head and arms, pelvis → legs) drawn on canvas. Each animation is a function of time that poses those bones, and switching crossfades between poses. Every body part and costume piece is drawn in the local space of the bone it belongs to — a hat in head space, a sleeve in arm space, a cape in torso space — so any piece follows any animation for free. Because every costume slot is independent, a knight's helmet, a wizard's robe and pirate boots compose without special cases. No image assets: everything is drawn.
