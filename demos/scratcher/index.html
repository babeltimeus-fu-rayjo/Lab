Scratch Card

Drag across the card to scratch the coating away. A picture is hiding underneath.

0%

The mechanic lives in scratcher.js next to this page: it lays a canvas coating over any element and erases it along the pointer's path (mouse, touch and pen). It reports how much is scratched and auto-reveals the rest once you pass the Reveal at threshold (adjustable above). Resizing the window keeps your scratches.
