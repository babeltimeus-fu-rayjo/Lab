Tip: click anywhere on the board to drop a single ball there.

The board lives in drop.js next to this page: a quincunx peg field on a canvas, with balls integrated under gravity and bounced off pegs and walls (with restitution and a dab of jitter so paths diverge). Balls settle into slots whose prize multipliers run hot toward the edges. Width, height and the balls-per-drop all feed setConfig(). Drop a few hundred and the counts trace a bell curve.
