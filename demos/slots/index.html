The reels and the spin live in slots.js next to this page. Each reel builds a strip of symbols, blurs, and eases to a stop under the payline with a staggered delay per reel — driven entirely by a CSS transform transition. Everything on the right calls setConfig() to rebuild the reels; there is no win logic at all.
