How much of a cell to scratch before it pops open.

The card lives in match.js next to this page. Every cell is its own mini scratch card — it reuses the same scratcher.js as the Scratch Card demo — hiding one symbol. Touching a cell commits one of your scratches (no free peeking); when the limit is spent, the rest lock. Reveal X of the same symbol anywhere on the card and it's a match. A predetermined Winning card always pays out: the match is scheduled to complete on a particular scratch, drawn anywhere between your X-th and your last, so it lands whichever cells you pick — but with scratches to spare it rarely lands on the first X, and falls somewhere different every game. A Losing card still holds a real match — it's just dealt into cells your budget can never reach, so Reveal all shows you exactly what you missed. That's why you can never scratch the whole card: the limit stops X cells short. Reveal at sets how far you must scratch an individual cell before it pops fully open.
