Card

A line needs 5 in a row.

The card lives in bingo.js next to this page. Every cell is its own mini scratch card — it reuses the same scratcher.js as the Scratch Card demo — hiding a ⭐ (a hit) or a dud. Touching a cell commits one of your scratches, so there's no free peeking; when the limit is spent, the rest lock. Complete any line of stars and it's a BINGO. A predetermined Winning card always hides a full star line (use Reveal all to see it); a Losing card never lets one complete.
